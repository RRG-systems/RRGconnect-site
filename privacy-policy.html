Privacy Policy

Last updated March 2026

1. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide when you submit forms on our website, including your full name, company name, email address, phone number, and any message or inquiry details. This information is collected for the purpose of responding to your request, communicating with you, and providing services related to revenue recovery, lead reactivation, recruiting, real estate inquiries, and related business support.

2. How We Use Information

We use the information you provide to respond to your inquiry, follow up regarding services, deliver requested information, improve our operations, and communicate with you about relevant service-related updates. We may also use submitted information to organize, route, and manage inquiries internally.

3. SMS Communications

By providing your phone number, you consent to receive text messages from M+RE Recruiting LLC dba The MORE Companies regarding your inquiry, services, and related updates. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. You may reply STOP to opt out and HELP for help. Consent is not a condition of purchase.

4. Sharing of Information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information with trusted service providers that help us operate our website, manage communications, or deliver services, but only to the extent reasonably necessary and subject to appropriate confidentiality obligations. We may also disclose information when required by law or to protect our legal rights.

5. Data Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards designed to protect your personal information against unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we take commercially reasonable steps to protect the information we collect.

6. Your Choices

You may contact us to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information, subject to applicable law. You may also opt out of SMS communications at any time by replying STOP to a text message you receive from us.

7. Contact Information

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or our data practices, please contact us through our website contact form.
